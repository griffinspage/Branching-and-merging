Our Mission

Nature is the foundation.
We are its architects.

EarthKeep was founded on a simple belief: that every ecosystem on Earth has intrinsic value, and that protecting biodiversity is not a luxury — it is a civilisational imperative. We work at the intersection of science, policy, and community action to halt habitat destruction, combat invasive species, and reverse the tide of species extinction.

Our approach is holistic. We don't just plant trees and walk away. We build long-term partnerships with indigenous communities, fund rigorous field research, and advocate for binding conservation legislation at national and international levels.

Flagship

The Great Rift Restoration

East Africa · 2019 – Present

Working across Kenya, Tanzania, and Uganda to restore over 200,000 hectares of savannah woodland, reintroduce native grasses, and support pastoral communities in sustainable land management.

Amazon Canopy Watch

South America · 2015 – Present

Real-time satellite monitoring of deforestation fronts and rapid-response legal interventions in partnership with Brazilian environmental agencies.

Coral Triangle Initiative

Southeast Asia · 2017 – Present

Reef restoration, bleaching monitoring, and community-run no-take zones across six island nations — protecting 30% of the world's coral reefs.

MarineReef Restoration

Northern Boreal Shield

Canada & Scandinavia · 2021 – Present

Protecting intact boreal forests — the world's largest land carbon sink — from industrial logging through legal designation and indigenous stewardship agreements.

Urban Rewilding Labs

Global Cities · 2022 – Present

Transforming urban spaces into functional wildlife habitats — pollinator corridors, native plantings, and urban wetlands — in partnership with 14 city governments.

Milestones
2011
EarthKeep founded in Nairobi with a team of 12 conservationists and a mandate to protect East African ecosystems.
2014
First Marine Protected Area established in the Lamu Archipelago, Kenya — protecting 45,000 sq km of ocean.
2017
Expanded to 20 countries; launched the Global Species Monitoring Network with 140 partner universities.
2020
Landmark 1 million hectares protected milestone. Received UN Environment Programme Award for Excellence.
2024
Partnered with 38 national governments on the Global Biodiversity Framework implementation strategy.
