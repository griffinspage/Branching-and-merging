Our Journey

13 Years of
Fighting for Nature

2011

Founded in Nairobi

Twelve conservationists. One mandate. EarthKeep launches with a $1.2M seed grant and a focus on East African savannahs.

🇰🇪 East Africa
2013

First 100K Hectares

Milestone: 100,000 hectares of Kenyan bush gazetted as protected land in partnership with the Kenya Wildlife Service.

🛡️ Land Protection
2015

Amazon Canopy Watch Launches

Our first international programme deploys satellite monitoring across 800,000 km² of Brazilian Amazon in real-time.

🌿 South America
2017

Coral Triangle Initiative

Partnership with six Southeast Asian nations establishes the world's largest coral reef monitoring network covering 30% of global reefs.

🌊 Marine
2019

Great Rift Restoration

Flagship restoration programme begins across Kenya, Tanzania, and Uganda targeting 200,000+ hectares of savannah woodland.

🦁 Wildlife
2020

1 Million Hectares Milestone

EarthKeep reaches one million protected hectares. Receives the UN Environment Programme Award for Excellence in Conservation.

🏆 Award
2022

Urban Rewilding Labs

Expansion into urban conservation — 14 city governments signed on to transform urban grey space into functional wildlife corridors.

🏙️ Urban
2026

38 Nations, 4.2M Hectares

Today. Over 4.2 million hectares protected across 38 countries. The mission continues with a $50M expansion plan for 2027–2030.
